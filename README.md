# Airship-LIFT-2026
Design of the systems involved in controlling, flying, and operating a scaled airship.

## Overview
Co-organized by FLYING WHALES and Planète Sciences, this annual competition invites teams of engineering students to take on an exciting challenge: to design an airship capable of carrying loads, starting from a blank envelope, essentially a mini version of the LCA60T!
Building upon the experience gained from the previous edition, this updated airship features key structural and mechanical upgrades to maximize payload efficiency, improve flight control, and ensure top performance across all competition challenges.

## Objectives
- Design and integration of a brand-new lightweight gondola to consolidate control, avionics, and power systems into a single optimized module
- Overhaul of the lifting and load-handling mechanism to boost winching reliability and payload stability
- Optimization of overall airship weight distribution and structural integrity to maximize usable lift

## Features
- 6 Meter length
- Reduced overall structural mass for improved payload capacity and maneuvering margin
- Fully integrated lightweight gondola housing all primary subsystems
- Redesigned sky crane and winching system for precise load pickup and transport

## Tools used
- 3DEXPERIENCE
- Fusion 360
- 3D printing

## My role
- Leading and managing overall project requirements and cross-functional engineering deliverables
- Structural design, manufacturing, and integration of the new lightweight gondola
- Redesign and testing of the internal lifting mechanism and winch assembly
- Systems integration, flight electronics, power, and propulsion control
